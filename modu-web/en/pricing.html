Pricing

Last updated: July 1, 2026

Current price

Modu is currently provided as a free, open-source toolkit and project website. Accessing this site, reading the documentation, viewing examples, and using code in the public repository do not currently require a subscription, license, or service fee.

Third-party costs

If you connect Modu to model providers, cloud services, messaging platforms, or other third-party tools in your own environment, those providers may charge under their own terms. Modu does not collect those charges, and they are not included in this free offering.

Refunds

Modu does not currently process payments or offer paid subscriptions on this site, so there is nothing for Modu to refund. Before launching any paid feature or commercial support plan, we will publish its price, billing period, cancellation process, and refund rules.

Support and contact

For pricing, commercial-use, or support questions, contact the maintainers at dev@crosszan.com. Do not send passwords, API keys, access tokens, or other sensitive data by email.
